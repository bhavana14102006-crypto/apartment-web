Our Story

BUILT ON
INTEGRITY

APARTment Group was founded in 2009 with a single conviction: that great architecture shapes great lives. What began as a boutique design-build firm has grown into one of the most respected names in elite urban construction.

Over 16 years we've delivered 34 landmark projects across 12 cities — from luxury residential towers to transformative mixed-use developments. Our portfolio of $256M reflects not just scale, but an uncompromising commitment to craft.

We believe buildings are more than structures — they're statements about what a community values, and promises to the people who will inhabit them for generations.
